AUDITABLE BY DESIGN

Matching, not guessing.

RoundyDot's first research model deliberately distinguishes commercial rules, observed prices, benchmark methodologies, regulatory disclosure and exchange increments.

01 /

Capture the original claim

One observation references a single dated publisher edition and clause/contract field. Store Product Scope, Price UOM, market, price stage, decimals, rounding and URL independently.

02 /

Normalize without distorting

USD 0.0001 per GAL equals US¢ 0.01 per GAL. This is a display conversion, not a second source. BBL, MT, m³ @15°C and m³ @20°C are never conflated without an explicit basis.

03 /

Do not mix evidence stages

A futures minimum tick describes an exchange quotation. It cannot certify the final physical unit price, no matter how many exchange instruments repeat it.

04 /

Require independent confirmation

FULL MATCH requires at least two independently originated, stage-comparable source families and agreement on the normalized increment. Two Shell entities alone do not meet the independence test.

05 /

Separate agreement from coverage

An empty source group is not a positive vote. With a single source, RoundyDot returns SINGLE SOURCE and with no evidence it returns NO EVIDENCE. It does not manufacture a confidence percentage.

06 /

Catalog first, verify next

CATALOGUED means an official publisher index lists the edition. CLAUSE REVIEWED means a specific priced rule was extracted. Links are pointers, not reproductions of copyrighted documents.
